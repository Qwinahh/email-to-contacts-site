Email to Contacts

Open an email, click Save to contacts, check the details read from the sender's signature, and save. The contact goes into your Google Contacts, and your phone shows it by itself (on iPhone, turn on Contacts for your Google account in Settings).

Privacy

The add-in reads only the email you have open, and only when you open it. It reads your contacts only to avoid saving the same person twice, and it writes only the contact you choose to save.

Everything runs in your Outlook app and talks directly to Google. Nothing is sent to, or stored by, anyone else.

To remove it, remove the add-in in Outlook (Get add-ins > My add-ins).
