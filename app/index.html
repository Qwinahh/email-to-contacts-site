Email to Contacts

Copy the person's signature from any email, paste it here, and save them to your phone's contacts.

iPhone

  1. In your email app, press and hold the signature, then Copy.
  2. Open this page and tap Paste from email, then Save to contacts.
  3. Tap Create New Contact (or Add to Contacts).

Tip: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen, to keep it one tap away.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome once and choose Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  2. In your email app, select the signature, tap Share, and choose Email to Contacts.
  3. Tap Save to contacts, then open the file to add the contact.

Private

Everything happens on your phone. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
