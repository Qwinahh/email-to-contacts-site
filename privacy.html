Email to Contacts

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Email to Contacts saves the sender of an email you're reading as a contact. It comes as a browser extension (Edge and Chrome), an Outlook add-in, and a phone app with a Share button. This policy covers all of them.

What it reads

  • Only the email you use it on, only when you click. It reads that email's sender and the message text to find their signature: name, email, phone numbers, job title and company. On other email websites, it reads only the text you select. On a phone, it reads only the text you share to it.
  • It never reads your other emails, your mailbox or your contacts.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere. The contact card is made on your own device and saved there. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that receives anything.
  • The Outlook add-in and the phone app open the Email to Contacts page with the details in the part of the link after #. Browsers never send that part to the web server, and the page removes it from the address once the card is made.
  • Nothing is stored apart from the contact card you choose to save. No advertising, profiling, selling, or training of AI models.

Removing it

Remove the extension from your browser's extensions page, the add-in from Outlook (Apps > Get add-ins > My add-ins), or the app from your phone. Contacts you saved stay until you delete them, like any other contact.
