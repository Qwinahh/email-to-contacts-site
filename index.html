Email to Contacts

Save the sender of an email to your contacts in one click: name, email, phone, job title and company, read from their signature. Free, no account, nothing sent anywhere.

iPhone

Add the Save to Contacts button
Then tap Add Shortcut.

The iPhone button is being finished. For now, open the app and tap Paste from email.

To use it: in any email app, press and hold the signature, select it, tap Share, then Save to Contacts.

Android

Open the app
Then tap Install (or ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen).

To use it: in any email app, select the signature, tap Share, then Email to Contacts.

Outlook (Windows app and Outlook on the web)

  1. Download the Outlook file
  2. Open Outlook's add-ins window, click My add-ins, scroll down, then Add a custom add-in > Add from File, and pick the file you downloaded.

To use it: open an email and click Save to contacts (under Apps; to keep it on every email, go to Settings > Mail > Customize actions and tick it).

Edge or Chrome (Gmail, Outlook.com, iCloud Mail, Yahoo)

  1. Download the extension, then right-click the file and choose Extract All.
  2. In the address bar, go to edge://extensions (Edge) or chrome://extensions (Chrome) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extracted email-to-contacts-extension folder.

To use it: open an email and click the blue Save to contacts button at the bottom right.

On a computer, the button saves a contact card. Open it to add the person: it goes into Contacts on a Mac (and your iPhone, with iCloud contacts on) or into Outlook on Windows.
